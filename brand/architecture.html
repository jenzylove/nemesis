EVIDENCE BOUNDARY — enforced in the data model, not in wording

Deterministic facts
Transactions, transfers, amounts, branch paths and attribution sources. Proved against the chain and stored before any model runs.
NEMESIS assessment
Gemini's reading of the compromise, carried separately with its own confidence and rejected if it names an entity the evidence cannot support.
Unknown and unresolved
Stated plainly. NEMESIS cannot freeze funds, recover funds, obtain account-holder identity, or compel any exchange or authority to act.
